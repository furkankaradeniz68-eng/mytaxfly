FAQ

Questions from agencies

Who is the MyTaxFly partner program for?

For businesses that serve owners of US LLCs, especially non-US founders: LLC formation agencies, tax advisors and accountants, registered agents and business consultants.

Can I use my own domain and brand?

Yes. Your clients log in on your own domain and see your brand name, logo and brand color in their dashboard.

Which IRS forms are supported?

Form 5472 with pro forma Form 1120 for foreign-owned single-member LLCs, Form 1065 with Schedule K-1 for multi-member LLCs, and Form 7004 extensions. Signed filings can be mailed to the IRS by USPS Certified Mail with tracking.

Can I connect my own software?

Yes. The Partner API lets you preview and submit filings, check their status and receive webhook notifications, with a test mode for development.

What does the partner program cost?

Conditions depend on the number of clients and the features you need. Contact us and we will put together an offer for your agency.

Does MyTaxFly provide tax advice?

No. MyTaxFly is software. Your clients and your team remain responsible for reviewing filings, ideally together with a licensed tax professional.
